Registration Page Validation:

The username input cannot be empty, less than 5 characters, or contain special characters
The email input cannot be empty, and must follow the pattern of "a@b.c"
The phone input cannot be empty, must be between 8 and 15 characters, and can only contain digits.

The first and second password inputs cannot be empty, they must match, password 1 must be at least 10 characters long, and contain at least 1 Uppercase letter, lowercase letter, number, and special character.

The Gender input must be selected

Country and Region cannot be empty

Must select at least 1 preferred genre

Limitations: 

email and phone checker only looks for the format, not for an actual valid email address or phone number

There is no check for valid countries or regions, so any input is accepted as long as the input isn't empty.

There are only 11 Genres, so any other preferred genres aren't selectable and there is no "none" option.

Booking Page Validation:

Full name cannot be empty
The email input cannot be empty, and must follow the pattern of "a@b.c"
The phone input cannot be empty, must be at least 10 digits, and can only contain numbers.

A movie must be chosen

A date and Time must be chosen
The screening date cannot be before the current date

Tickets cannot be a less than or equal to 0

you must choose a payment method (credit card or voucher)
if Online payment is selected:
credit card number cannot be empty, can only contain digits and must be the same as the chosen providers number length.
expiry date cannot be empty and must eb in the format MM / YYYY (a number between 1-12, a forward or back slash, and 4 digits)

the expiry date cannot be before the current date

the CVV cannot be empty, can only contain digits, and must be either 3 or 4 digits depending on card provider.

Billing email input cannot be empty, and must follow the pattern of "a@b.c"

Limitations: 

email and phone checker only looks for the format, not for an actual valid email address or phone number

Card details are not validated just the format, and no payment is processed (In line with requirements)

billing email also only looks for the format, not a valid email address.



General Limitations: 

Session dates are hardcoded and don't go away automatically once the session date has passed.

ticket types can only be assigned for each transaction, not for each ticket bought, so buying 3 tickets can only be assigned one ticket type, not 3 individual types. 

I only made 6 of the movies bookable because I was too lazy to add the rest of them. The other 13 are only viewable on the recommendation page.
